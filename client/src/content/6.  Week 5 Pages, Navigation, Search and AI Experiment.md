# 📅 Week 5: Pages, Navigation, Search and AI Experiments (full step-by-step guide)

# **Week goal:**

finish the About, FAQ and Contact pages and all store policies, build the full main and footer navigation, set up Search & Discovery (filters, synonyms, boosts and search settings), and run a set of AI experiments with the results written up in a table.

**Time needed:** about 1.5–2 hours a day for 7 days.

I checked this against Shopify’s Help Center, the Winter '26 Edition announcement and recent Shopify Community threads. **Six corrections to what I told you earlier:**

- **Sidekick can now edit your theme settings.** Since the Winter '26 Edition, you can click any element in the theme editor and ask Sidekick to change it (for example, “make this button rounded”). It changes global styles or block settings. **My earlier prediction that it “can’t edit the storefront” is out of date.** You’ll test exactly what it can and can’t do on Day 6.
- **“Shop by Concern” links go to the concern collections from Week 4**, not to tag filters or search results.
- **Search & Discovery is already installed** (Week 4), and complementary products are already set. This week you only check them.
- **Filters:** you can have **up to 25 filters.** Skin type works as a filter because it’s a **list of single-line text**. Size comes from the **product option.**
- **Semantic (meaning-based) search needs the Grow plan or higher.** Your dev store is on Basic, so **synonyms and boosts matter even more.**
- **Your return policy must match what you’ve already promised.** The Week 4 announcement said “no questions asked,” but hygiene rules for skincare make that hard to promise. You’ll fix the wording on Day 3.

# 🟢 Day 2: FAQ Page and Contact Page

## 2.1 🤖 Generate a first draft with Sidekick

**Paste this into Sidekick:**

```
Write 10 FAQ questions and answers for SOLENNE, a fictional botanical
skincare brand. Group them under: Shipping, Returns, Ingredients & skin,
Orders & bundles. Facts to use: orders ship in 1–2 business days;
standard delivery 3–7 business days; free shipping over $60, otherwise $6;
returns of unused products within 30 days of delivery; the Daily Routine
Set contains Gentle Cleanser 150 ml, Hydrating Serum 30 ml and Barrier
Cream 50 ml and saves $15. Include a question on patch testing and one on
how long products last after opening. Calm, honest tone. Max 50 words per
answer. No medical claims, no "cures", "treats", "heals" or "clinically proven".
```

**Paste the draft into your log** as the “Sidekick FAQ v1.”

## 2.2 Edit checklist

- ☐ **Every fact matches the store**: shipping rates, return window and bundle contents
- ☐ **No medical claims**
- ☐ **Answers are 50 words or fewer**
- ☐ **Patch testing is described safely**, including advice to stop using the product and see a doctor if irritation happens
- ☐ **The tone is calm**, without hype

## 2.3 Final FAQ (use this version)

**Shipping**

**1. How long does shipping take?**

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days, and you’ll get a tracking link by email.

**2. Is shipping free?**

Yes, on orders over $60. Orders under $60 have a flat $6 standard shipping rate.

**Returns**

**3. What is your return policy?**

You can return unused products within 30 days of delivery. If a product doesn’t suit your skin, contact us and we’ll help find a solution.

**4. How do I start a return?**

Email [hello@solenne-demo.com](mailto:hello@solenne-demo.com) with your order number. We’ll reply within one business day with the next steps.

**Ingredients & skin**

**5. Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?**

Our formulas are gentle and made with sensitive skin in mind, but every skin is different. Check the ingredients on each product page and patch test before first use.

**6. How do I patch test?**

Apply a small amount to your inner arm and wait 24–48 hours. If you notice redness or irritation, don’t use the product. If irritation continues, speak to a doctor or pharmacist.

**7. Are your products cruelty-free?**

Yes. Our products and ingredients are never tested on animals.

**8. How long do products last after opening?**

Look for the open-jar symbol on the pack. It shows how many months a product is best used within after opening. Store products somewhere cool and dry, away from direct sunlight.

**Orders & bundles**

**9. What’s in the Daily Routine Set?**

Gentle Cleanser (150 ml), Hydrating Serum (30 ml) and Barrier Cream (50 ml). It covers a full daily routine and saves $15 compared with buying each product separately.

**10. Can I change or cancel my order?**

Contact us as soon as possible. We can change or cancel an order until it has shipped.

# 🟢 Day 5: Search & Discovery

## 5.1 Check that the theme supports filters

**Horizon supports filters.** To check any theme: look under **Content → Menus** (or **Online Store → Navigation** in older admins). If a theme doesn’t support filtering, a message appears in the **Collection and search filters** section.

## 5.2 Filters

**Apps → Search & Discovery → Filters → Add filter:**

| # | Source | Label customers see | Logic | Sort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Availability** | In stock | — | — |
| 2 | **Price** | Price | — | — |
| 3 | **Product type** | Product type | OR | Automatic |
| 4 | **Metafield: Skin type** (`custom.skin_type`) | Skin type | **OR** | **Manual** (see below) |
| 5 | **Product option: Size** | Size | OR | Automatic |

**Manual sort for Skin type:** All skin types → Dry → Normal → Combination → Oily → Sensitive

**Settings → Filters → Empty values:** choose **Hide**, so shoppers never see an option that leads to zero products.

**Why OR logic:** choosing “Dry” **and** “Sensitive” should show products for **either** skin type. AND logic would narrow the results too far for such a small catalog.

**Remove the default filters you don’t need** (such as Vendor, since every product is SOLENNE, and Tags, which are internal codes like `step:1`).

## 5.3 Filter layout in the theme

**Customize → Collections → Default collection → Main collection:**

- **Filter position:** a **drawer on mobile**; on desktop, a **horizontal bar** (it suits small catalogs) or a **sidebar**
- **Show the product count**
- **Sorting:** on

**Also: Customize → page selector → Search:** apply the **same grid and filter settings** as the collection page (2 columns on mobile, 3 on desktop).

## 5.4 Synonyms

**Search & Discovery → Search → Synonyms → Create synonym group:**

| # | Synonym group |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | moisturizer, moisturiser, cream, face cream, moisturising cream |
| 2 | serum, essence, booster |
| 3 | cleanser, face wash, facial wash, cleansing gel |
| 4 | face oil, facial oil, oil |
| 5 | set, bundle, kit, routine set, gift set |

**How it works:** within a group, **searching for any term finds products that match the others.** Someone searching “face wash” finds the Gentle Cleanser.

**Don’t add simple misspellings or plurals.** Shopify handles those automatically.

## 5.5 Product boosts

**Search & Discovery → Search → Product boosts → Create product boost:**

| Product | Search terms (maximum 10 per product) |
| --- | --- |
| **Hydrating Serum** | hydration, hydrating, dry skin, hyaluronic acid, dehydrated |
| **Barrier Cream** | sensitive skin, ceramides, barrier, dry patches |
| **Botanical Face Oil** | glow, radiance, dull skin, rosehip |
| **Daily Routine Set** | gift, starter, routine, beginner |

**Good to know:**

- **Only in-stock products are boosted.** Sold-out products go to the end of the results.
- **Boosts don’t apply** when a search uses special search syntax
- **Boost one product, or only a few, per term.** Too many boosts make the results worse.

## 5.6 Search settings

| **Predictive search shows** | Products, Collections, Pages, Suggested searches |
| **Out-of-stock products** | **Show last** |

**Note:** these settings can be **overridden by the theme** if the theme requests specific result types.

# 🟢 Day 6: 🤖 AI Lab

## 6.1 Set up a safe test theme

1. **Online Store → Themes → Duplicate** the live theme
2. **Rename it:** `AI Lab — DO NOT PUBLISH`
3. **Run every experiment in this copy**, never in the live theme

**Why:** AI-generated blocks are saved as **files in the theme’s `/blocks` folder**, and Sidekick can change **global settings**. **Keep your finished brand system safe.**

## 6.2 Scorecard (use it for every test)

| Criterion | What to check | Score 1–5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Works** | Does it do what you asked? |  |
| **Desktop and mobile** | Layout, stacking, spacing |  |
| **Brand system** | Does it use your **palette colors and theme fonts**, or hard-coded values? |  |
| **Settings** | Can a merchant edit it in the editor? |  |
| **Accessibility** | Keyboard, contrast, alt text, heading order |  |
| **Code quality** | Open the file in `/blocks`: length, inline styles, fixed colors, JavaScript |  |
| **Time** | Minutes from prompt to a usable result |  |

## 6.3 Test A: Magic block, simple (trust badges)

**Steps:** Customize → click a section that **accepts theme blocks** (for example an empty **Section**) → **Add block** → **Generate** → paste the prompt → **Generate** → preview → **Keep** or **Discard**

**Prompt:**

```
A trust badges block with 4 items in a row: icon, short title, one-line
text. Items: Cruelty-free / Never tested on animals; Recyclable glass /
Made to be reused; 30-day returns / On unused products; Free shipping /
On orders over $60. Stack 2x2 on mobile. Include settings to edit each
title, text and icon color. Use the theme's fonts and colors.
```

**Then check:**

- ☐ Does it look like SOLENNE, or like a generic widget?
- ☐ **Open the code:** Edit code → `/blocks` → find the new file. **Are the colors fixed hex values or palette settings?**
- ☐ **Try editing it with a new AI prompt after saving.** Users report you can’t. **Record what happens.**

## 6.4 Test B: Magic block, complex (routine steps with add-to-cart)

**Prompt:**

```
A routine steps block showing 3 numbered steps side by side (Cleanse,
Treat, Moisturize). Each step has a product picker setting, and shows the
product image, step number, product name, short text and an "Add to cart"
button that adds the product without leaving the page and updates the
cart drawer. Stack vertically on mobile.
```

**Then check:**

- ☐ Can you **choose products** in the settings?
- ☐ Does **Add to cart** work **without reloading the page**?
- ☐ Does the **cart drawer open and update** (item count, items)?
- ☐ Can you use it with the **keyboard**? Is the button a real `<button>`?
- ☐ **What happens with an out-of-stock product?**

## 6.5 Test C: Sidekick editing in the theme editor (new since Winter '26)

**In the AI Lab theme’s editor, click an element, then ask Sidekick:**

| # | Click on | Ask Sidekick | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Hero button | “Make this button rounded” | **Did it change only this button, or every button in the global settings?** |
| C2 | Hero | “Change this image to the serum texture photo” | Can it pick files from Content → Files? |
| C3 | Announcement bar | “Change the text to ‘New: the Daily Routine Set’” | Is it quick and accurate? |
| C4 | Best sellers | “Show 3 products per row on desktop” | Did it find the right setting? |
| C5 | Any section | “Add a section with a quote from our founder” | **Does it generate a new block in a new section?** |
| C6 | Product page | “Show the Key ingredients as small pill-shaped badges” | **Can it do something that needs new code?** |

**The key lesson from C1:** Sidekick **changes settings.** If it changes a **global** setting, it can **break your brand system without you noticing** (for example, every button becomes rounded). **That’s a real risk for merchants, and a reason they hire you to keep the system consistent.**

**About C5:** blocks Sidekick generates in a new section are wrapped in a Shopify-generated section (`_blocks.liquid`). **In Stage 3 you’ll learn to customize that wrapper.**
