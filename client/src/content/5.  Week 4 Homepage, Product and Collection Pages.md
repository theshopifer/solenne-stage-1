# 📅 Week 4: Homepage, Product and Collection Pages (full step-by-step guide)

# **Week goal:**

a finished homepage, one product template that works for all 4 products through dynamic sources, a separate bundle template, and collection pages with banners. Everything should look professional on mobile and desktop.

**Time needed:** about 1.5–2 hours a day for 7 days.

I checked this against Horizon’s section list, Shopify’s Help Center and recent Shopify Community threads. **Five corrections to what I told you earlier:**

- **Complementary products need the Search & Discovery app.** Without it, the “Complete your routine” block stays empty, so you install the app now (Day 1) instead of in Week 5. Filters still wait until Week 5.
- **“Shop by concern” needs its own collections.** You add 3 concern collections, plus a Best sellers collection, to the 3 category collections, making **7 collections** in total.
- **Where to connect metafields in Horizon:** open the Accordion → Accordion row → the **Text block inside it**, then click the small **database icon**. **Empty metafields still show an empty row**, which is why the bundle gets its own template (Day 6).
- **“Match any condition” has a trap.** Only use type conditions with it. A *product status* condition under “any” adds every product with that status to the collection.
- **Section padding is one value per section.** Set it once and check mobile. Horizon usually scales it down on small screens, but confirm it looks right.

## 5.4 How to connect a dynamic source (step by step)

1. Click the **Text** block
2. Find the **Text** field in the sidebar
3. Click the **database icon** (“Connect dynamic source”) next to the field
4. Choose the metafield (for example **Tagline**)
5. **Delete any leftover placeholder text** in the field, **or it will show twice**
6. Save

**Can’t see the metafield in the list?**

- Scroll down, since the list can be long
- Check the definition: **Settings → Custom data → Products → the metafield → Storefronts access: On**
- **Check the type.** Text fields accept text-type metafields. A **list** metafield may display with commas between the values, so **preview how it looks.**
- Refresh the theme editor
